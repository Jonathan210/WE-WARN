WE–WARN

Water Elevation Web-based Alert and Response Network

A web-based system that continuously monitors the current water level at Dr. A. Santos Ave. infront of SM Sucat, Barangay San Dionisio. It provides real-time water level readings, warning status (Green / Yellow / Red), and road passability information to help residents make safer travel and evacuation decisions during heavy rainfall and flooding.
